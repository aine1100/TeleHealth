
# Alive Health UG

> Your health, your choice, your terms

Alive Health UG is a B2B SaaS telehealth platform for Uganda that connects patients, doctors, clinics, pharmacies, laboratories, insurers, and administrators in one ecosystem.

This repository contains the initial implementation of the platform and a working product guide for future development.

---

## Project Purpose

The platform is designed to support:

- Online doctor consultations
- Appointment booking and waiting-room flow
- Mobile Money payments
- Medicine reminders
- Medical record management
- AI-assisted symptom and vital screening
- Pharmacy and lab integration
- Insurance workflows
- Admin and business oversight

---
